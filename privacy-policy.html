Privacy Policy for Layover Friends

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Layover Friends ("we," "us," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name and email address (via Google Sign-In)
  • Profile information you choose to provide
  • Google account details for authentication

2.2 Travel Information

We collect information about your layovers and travel plans:

  • Flight details (airport, arrival/departure times)
  • Layover duration
  • Travel interests and preferences

2.3 Communication Data

When you use our chat features, we store:

  • Messages you send to other users
  • Timestamps of conversations

2.4 Device Information

We may collect:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version and crash reports

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Connect you with other travelers during layovers
  • Provide flight tracking and notification services
  • Improve and personalize the App
  • Send important notifications about your account
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase Cloud Firestore. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Authentication: For secure login
  • Firebase Firestore: For data storage
  • AviationStack API: For flight tracking information
  • Groq AI: For AI-powered recommendations and features

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With other users (your profile information for matching purposes)
  • With service providers who help us operate the App
  • When required by law or to protect our rights

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt-out of non-essential communications

8. Retention of Data

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where required by law.

9. Children's Privacy

Layover Friends is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and will request your consent if required by law.
